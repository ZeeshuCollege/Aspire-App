Fee Disclosure & Refund Policy

ASPIRE Learning Centre • Transparent Academic Fee Guidelines • Version 1.0

1. Overview & Policy Objective

At ASPIRE Learning Centre, we believe in complete financial transparency with students and parents. This Fee Disclosure & Refund Policy outlines the fee structures, inclusions, refund eligibility criteria, cancellation procedures, and disbursement timelines for all secondary coaching, higher secondary, and competitive entrance preparation programs (Class 9th, 10th, 11th, 12th Science, NEET, JEE, and MHT-CET).

2. Transparent Fee Inclusions & Zero Hidden Charges

Clear Disclosure: The tuition fee quoted at admission is comprehensive and covers all standard educational services. There are no hidden platform maintenance charges, surprise assessment fees, or compulsory digital device fees.

Your academic fee covers:

  • Classroom Instruction: Complete offline syllabus lectures by verified faculty.
  • Printed Study Kit: High-yield theory booklets, question banks, and revision notes.
  • Test Series: Regular chapter-wise, unit, and cumulative mock test papers with detailed performance evaluations.
  • Doubt Clearing: Structured faculty doubt-solving sessions.
  • Digital Ecosystem: 24/7 access to the ASPIRE Learning App for digital timetables, attendance tracking, test reports, and parent communication.

3. Fee Components

  • Registration & Admission Processing Fee: A non-refundable allocation fee (maximum 10% of total course fee or ₹2,500, whichever is lower) utilized for student seat holding, batch allocation, and printing of initial kits.
  • Tuition Fee: The instructional component of the fee, which is refundable strictly on a pro-rata basis as detailed in Section 4 below.

4. Refund Eligibility & Deduction Schedule

Refund calculations are based strictly on the formal date of written application submission at the institute desk or via registered email from the parent:

Time of Cancellation Request Tuition Fee Refund Percentage Deduction Specifics
More than 15 calendar days before batch start 100% of Tuition Fee Registration / admission processing fee only.
1 to 14 days before batch start date 85% of Tuition Fee 15% administrative and materials preparation deduction.
Within first 14 calendar days of batch start Pro-Rata Tuition Balance (approx. 60-70%) Cost of classes conducted up to request date + printed study material kit charges.
Between 15 and 30 days of batch start 35% of Tuition Fee Pro-rata attendance deduction, materials cost, and faculty resource reservations.
After 30 calendar days from batch start Non-Refundable (0%) Classroom seats and faculty commitments are locked for the academic session.

5. Non-Refundable Items & Special Circumstances

  • Printed study materials, uniform kits, or laboratory access materials once handed over are non-refundable for hygiene and copyright reasons.
  • Special consideration may be granted at the Institute Director's sole discretion in verifiable cases of medical emergency or student family relocation with appropriate documentation.

6. How to Apply for a Refund

  1. The parent or legal guardian must submit a signed Fee Refund Request Form at the ASPIRE administrative desk or via email to aspirelearningcentre@outlook.com.
  2. Include the original fee payment receipt number, student roll number, enrolled batch, reason for withdrawal, and verified parent bank account details (Bank Name, Account Holder Name, Account Number, and IFSC Code).
  3. Upon receipt, the administration will issue a written acknowledgment and compute the settlement within 7 business days.

7. Mode & Timeline of Refund Disbursement

All approved refunds are disbursed strictly via direct electronic bank transfer (NEFT / RTGS) or account payee cheque in favor of the parent/guardian whose name appears on the admission application.

Disbursement Timeline: Approved refunds are processed and transferred within 15 to 30 working days from the date of final audit approval. No cash refunds are distributed.
