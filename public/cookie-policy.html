Cookie & Local Storage Policy

ASPIRE Learning Centre • Transparent Browser Storage Practices • Version 1.0

1. What Are Cookies & Local Storage?

Cookies and local storage technologies (such as HTML5 localStorage and sessionStorage) are small data files and key-value records saved in your browser or WebView when you access the ASPIRE Learning Centre web and mobile applications. They allow our platform to maintain your authenticated login session, preserve your display preferences, and securely manage educational data.

Zero Third-Party Advertising: ASPIRE Learning Centre does NOT use commercial advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party behavioral profiling trackers. All storage is strictly dedicated to essential academic functions and platform performance.

2. Categories of Storage We Use

Category Purpose Examples Required?
Strictly Necessary Maintains student/parent authentication, role access control, security lockout rate limiting, and encrypted session continuity. aspire_is_logged_in, aspire_user_role, sb-auth-token Mandatory (App cannot function without these)
Functional & Preferences Remembers UI preferences, notice board read states, and your saved cookie preference choices. aspire_cookie_consent_v1, aspire_notices_list Optional
Diagnostics & Cache Locally buffers offline study material references and captures runtime errors in the Error Boundary to prevent crashes. aspire_offline_cache, Local Error Logs Optional

3. How to Manage Your Cookies & Storage Choices

You have full control over your stored preferences:

  • In-App Cookie Banner & Legal Centre: You can review and adjust your preference choices at any time directly through our in-app Legal Centre or Cookie Preferences manager.
  • Browser Settings: You can configure your browser (Google Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Microsoft Edge) to refuse cookies or clear local storage. Note that clearing strictly necessary storage will log you out of the student/parent portal.
  • Mobile Device Settings: On Android, you can clear cached application data via Settings → Apps → ASPIRE Learning → Storage & cache → Clear cache.

4. Updates to this Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy periodically to reflect technological improvements or legal compliance requirements. The date of the latest update will always be noted at the top of this document.
