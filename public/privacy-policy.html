Privacy Policy

ASPIRE Learning Centre • Effective Date: October 1, 2026 • Version 1.0

1. Introduction & Scope

ASPIRE Learning Centre ("we", "us", or "our") is dedicated to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and security of our students, parents, and faculty. This Privacy Policy details our data collection, processing, storage, and retention practices across our mobile applications, web portals, and offline coaching management operations.

This policy is drafted to comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 (India), the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and Google Play Developer Policies & Data Safety requirements.

2. Data We Collect & Specific Purpose

We adhere to strict data minimization principles. We collect only data strictly necessary for academic administration and student safety:

Data Category Specific Items Educational Purpose Mandatory / Optional
Identity Information Full Name, Roll Number, Enrolled Course/Batch (e.g. JEE, NEET, 10th). Account identification, batch allocation, and student attendance ledger. Mandatory for enrolled students

Zero Commercial Advertising: We do NOT sell, rent, broker, or monetize student or parent data. We do NOT use advertising identifiers (AAID) and do NOT display third-party advertisements in our software.

3. Device Permissions & Purpose

The ASPIRE Learning mobile application requests only the minimal system permissions required for core educational features:

  • INTERNET: Essential for secure communication with our backend database (over TLS 1.3), syncing attendance, and downloading study PDFs.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): Alerts students and parents regarding upcoming lectures, schedule changes, test results, attendance status, and fee updates.
  • CAMERA (Optional): Invoked solely if the user voluntarily captures a new profile photo. It is never activated in the background.
  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES (Optional): Invoked solely if the user selects an existing photo from their local gallery for their profile picture.

Explicit Exclusions: We do NOT request or access GPS location, microphone, audio recordings, contact address books, or external phone logs.

4. Third-Party Service Providers & Data Security

We work exclusively with trusted, enterprise-grade infrastructure providers operating under strict confidentiality:

  • Supabase Inc. — Database hosting, authentication, and secure data storage hosted on AWS SOC 2 compliant infrastructure. All data in transit is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS), and database access is enforced via PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS).
  • Meta Cloud API (WhatsApp Business) — Used exclusively to dispatch one-time passwords (OTPs) to verified phone numbers for password recovery and account verification.

For a detailed breakdown of all libraries and licensing, view our Open Source Licenses & IP Credits.

5. Children & Minors Privacy

Our enrolled students include teenagers (aged 13 to 18) preparing for secondary and competitive entrance exams, as well as adult learners and parents.

  • We collect personal data for students under 18 strictly with verified parental or guardian consent during coaching admission.
  • Parent accounts are explicitly linked to their child's account so that parents can supervise academic notices, attendance, marks, and fees at all times.
  • No open social forums, direct messaging between strangers, or behavioral profiling exist in the application.

6. Account Deletion & Right to Erasure

In accordance with Google Play developer policies and Section 12 of the DPDP Act 2023, users have the right to request the permanent deletion of their account and personal data at any time.

In-App Deletion Path:

Online Web Deletion Portal:

7. Communication Preferences & Unsubscribe

You may opt out of receiving promotional announcements, newsletters, or workshop invitations at any time via our self-service Communication Preferences & Unsubscribe Portal. Essential academic notices (attendance alerts and exam marks) remain active for enrolled students to ensure academic safety.

8. Cookies & Local Storage

Our web application uses essential cookies and local storage tokens strictly for session authentication and preference management. We do not use third-party tracking cookies. For full details, review our Cookie Policy.
